Why Allergyze exists

From fear to finally knowing.

For years, people with contact allergies navigate the beauty aisle in fear — reacting to products, unable to figure out why, avoiding anything unfamiliar.

Patch testing changes everything. You finally have your list. But then what? Reading ingredient labels is a full-time job, and even the best apps weren't built for patch-tested patients.

Allergyze was. Every result is weighted by your actual reaction score — because a +3 steroid allergy isn't the same as a +1 lanolin sensitivity, and you deserve to know the difference.
